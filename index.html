Modern Farming Methods

Technology that raises yield and saves resources.

🏠

Greenhouse Farming

Crops grow in a controlled environment, giving protection from weather and pests and allowing year-round production.

💧

Hydroponics

Plants grow in nutrient-rich water without soil. It uses up to 90% less water and suits leafy greens and tomatoes.

🏢

Vertical Farming

Crops are stacked in layers indoors with LED lighting, ideal for cities with little land.

🛰️

Precision Farming

GPS, sensors and drones guide exact seed, water and fertilizer use for each part of the field.

🌿

Organic Farming

Uses compost, green manure and biological pest control instead of synthetic chemicals.

📱

IoT & Mobile Apps

Soil moisture sensors and phone apps send alerts for irrigation, weather and market prices.

Fertilizers

Feed your crop the right nutrients.

N

Nitrogen (N)

Promotes leafy green growth. Sources: Urea (46% N), Ammonium sulphate.

P

Phosphorus (P)

Strengthens roots and helps flowering. Sources: DAP, Single Super Phosphate.

K

Potassium (K)

Improves disease resistance and fruit quality. Sources: Muriate of Potash (60% K₂O).

🐄

Organic Manure

Farmyard manure, compost and vermicompost improve soil structure and microbial life.

⚠️ Always apply fertilizer based on a soil test. Over-fertilizing wastes money and harms soil and water.

Farmer Tips

  • Rotate crops every season to break pest cycles and restore soil nutrients.
  • Mulch around plants to hold moisture and suppress weeds.
  • Water early in the morning to reduce evaporation loss.
  • Use certified seeds and treat them before sowing.
  • Keep farm records of inputs, costs and yields to plan better.
